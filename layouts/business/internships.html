Axiom Asia Private CapitalAbout the firm

Axiom Asia Private Capital is a Singapore-headquartered private equity investment firm founded in 2006, specializing in fund-of-funds, co-investments and secondary investments. It focuses exclusively on the Asia-Pacific private equity market, providing global institutional investors with diversified access to mid-market buyout, growth and venture opportunities across the region. A key milestone was the US$1.28 billion closing of its seventh flagship fund-of-funds, Axiom Asia 7, in 2025, bringing the firm’s cumulative fund commitments to more than US$9 billion.
